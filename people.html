Principal Investigator

Dr. Longsheng Feng
Assistant Professor, Mechanical Engineering · Wayne State University
Email: longsheng.feng@wayne.edu
Research interests: microstructure evolution in extreme environments; mesoscale/phase-field modeling; stacking faults & defect-mediated deformation; microstructure-aware design of energy/structural materials; data-driven PMP.
Dr. Feng’s group develops physics-based and data-driven tools to connect processing, emergent microstructure, and properties in demanding environments, spanning high-temperature alloys and architected energy materials.
