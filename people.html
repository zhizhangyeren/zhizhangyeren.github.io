Bio:
Mubasher joined M2D2 lab as a Ph.D. student in Fall 2026. He received his M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS) and his B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore. His past research combines computational fluid dynamics, multiphase flow modeling, turbulence modeling, heat transfer, and phase transformation phenomena to better understand complex thermal-fluid systems. His current research focuses on mesoscale chemo-mechanical degradation modeling battery materials. In his free time, he enjoys badminton and cricket.
