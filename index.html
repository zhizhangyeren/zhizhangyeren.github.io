Our lab advances materials for energy and extreme environments by decoding how microstructure governs properties and by unraveling the Processing–Microstructure–Property relationship. We build multiphysics computational tools and digital twins that capture how microstructures form, evolve, and degrade under operating conditions, and provide guidance for materials design.
